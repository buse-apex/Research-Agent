# 3.9.0: Evidence-led school research

Built from the supplied v38 app. The original project folder name is retained for compatibility.

## Added

Modular discovery, bounded public-page reads, text-PDF extraction, relevant linked pages, Facebook signal selection, structured receipts/dates, fact review, needs/JTBD hypotheses, alternatives/counterevidence, discovery questions and relationship-aware reviewed drafts.

Owner context fields and a complete evidence brief with HTML download. Older brief rendering and history remain available.

## Corrected

Facebook `profile.php?id=` handling; prior-spring exclusion; signals lost through early truncation; undated posts treated as recent; implied exhaustive coverage; automatically confirmed official sources; old events assigned current years; uniform outreach; financial scope; and elementary assumptions for mixed campuses.

Direct reads validate public addresses on redirects and pin checked DNS addresses. Retrieved instructions are treated as untrusted content. Reads have size/time limits.

Unused old research modules were removed. The quick meeting check remains separate. Next.js was updated to 14.2.35; extraction, validation and test dependencies were added. A lockfile is included.

## Delivery boundaries

Source-code delivery, not deployment. No credentials included. No automatic emailing, CRM writes, personality diagnosis, recurring research or book revision. Needs remain hypotheses pending conversation.

Model defaults have environment overrides. Use models supported by your account and reevaluate quality after changing them.
